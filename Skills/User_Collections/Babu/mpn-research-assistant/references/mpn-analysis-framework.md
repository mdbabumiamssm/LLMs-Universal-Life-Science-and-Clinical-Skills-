# MPN Analysis Framework

## Core framing questions

- Disease entity and stage.
- Driver mutation status and allele burden.
- Co-mutation profile and cytogenetics.
- Fibrosis grade, symptom burden, and treatment history.
- Sample type: peripheral blood, bone marrow, CD34+, megakaryocyte-enriched, plasma.

## Organize outputs into five blocks

1. Disease context.
2. Molecular findings.
3. Cell-state or pathway interpretation.
4. Clinical or translational implications.
5. Data gaps and next experiments.

## Recurrent themes worth checking

- JAK-STAT activation and inflammatory signaling.
- Megakaryocyte differentiation state and platelet-bias programs.
- Fibrosis-associated cytokines and extracellular matrix remodeling.
- Clonal evolution, TP53 or PPM1D stress-response biology, and therapy selection pressure.
- Divergence between marrow and blood readouts.

## Good output pattern

- Short claim.
- Evidence source or dataset.
- Confidence level.
- Why it matters for the specific project.
- What to test next.
